THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

Hello,
I’m Louise.

LA

Wedding days should feel like your day.

Based in Bridgend, Louise photographs weddings and families across South Wales and travels for wedding celebrations.

Her style centres on natural, candid moments, with traditional portraits woven in. She follows what matters to you and helps you feel at ease, keeping the photographs personal and the experience relaxed.

Louise brings a friendly, practical presence to a wedding: there to help with the little things as well as capture them. Flowers, dancing and cake are among her favourite parts of the celebrations.

BEFORE YOUR DAY

A few helpful
answers.

The essentials, with the finer details confirmed when you enquire.

Have another question? ↗
Where is Louise based?

Bridgend, South Wales. Louise photographs weddings and families.

Can Louise travel for a wedding?

Yes, including Wales and England. Further travel and any overnight stay are arranged directly with Louise; ask for a quote for your location.

How far ahead can I enquire?

Her supplier profile advises enquiries up to two years ahead. Message Louise to confirm your date.

Does Louise photograph more than one wedding a day?

No, according to her public supplier profile.

How will I receive my photographs?

Edited photographs are available in an online gallery. Print, USB and album inclusions depend on the package you agree.

When will my photographs be ready?

The supplier listing gives a delivery estimate of two months. Confirm the current timescale with Louise when booking.

How do I check prices and availability?

Share your date, venue and preferred coverage. Louise will confirm availability and provide a current quote.
